Why retrieval matters

When you write about a technical topic, you often recall fragments of what you have read before. The system will receive your draft and compare it with the sources, so the conclusion you draw is grounded rather than guessed.

In practice, an obscure word like gerrymander can remind you that precision matters: choose the term you actually mean.
